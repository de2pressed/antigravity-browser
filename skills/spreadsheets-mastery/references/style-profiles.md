# Spreadsheet Style Profiles & Visual Hierarchy

Executive presentations demand high visual craft. A beautiful spreadsheet commands attention, signals technical competence, and improves analytical clarity.

---

## 1. Complete Style Profiles

### Profile 1: Classic Navy (Investment Banking & Private Equity)
- **Primary Header Fill**: `#1B365D` (Dark Navy)
- **Header Text Color**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White, Bold)
- **Accent Line / Subheaders**: `#4A90E2` (Soft Steel Blue)
- **Zebra Row Alternation**: `#F8F9FA` / `#FFFFFF`
- **Total Row Fill**: `#EAEFF5` with a thin top border and double bottom border
- **Font**: Arial, 10pt (Headers 11pt bold, Title 14pt bold)

### Profile 2: Slate Minimal (Silicon Valley SaaS & Engineering)
- **Primary Header Fill**: `#212529` (Charcoal Black)
- **Header Text Color**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White, Bold)
- **Accent / Metric Highlights**: `#495057`
- **Zebra Row Alternation**: `#F8F9FA` / `#FFFFFF`
- **Summary Row Fill**: `#E9ECEF`
- **Font**: Inter or Segoe UI, 10pt (clean monospace feel for numbers)

### Profile 3: Modern Emerald (Operations, Supply Chain & CleanTech)
- **Primary Header Fill**: `#064E3B` (Deep Emerald Spruce)
- **Header Text Color**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White, Bold)
- **Accent / Highlight**: `#059669` (Vibrant Jade)
- **Zebra Row Alternation**: `#F0FDF4` / `#FFFFFF`
- **Summary Row Fill**: `#D1FAE5`
- **Font**: DM Sans or Calibri, 10pt

---

## 2. Border Hierarchy Rules

Borders should be used sparingly to define structure, not to enclose every single cell in a black cage:

1. **Table Header**:
   - Top border: None (header fill defines the boundary).
   - Bottom border: 2px solid dark accent.
2. **Body Rows**:
   - Horizontal interior gridlines: Thin light gray (`#E0E0E0` or `#E5E7EB`).
   - Vertical interior gridlines: Omit or use very faint gray. Let whitespace separate columns.
3. **Subtotal Rows**:
   - Top border: 1px solid medium gray (`#9E9E9E`).
   - Bottom border: None.
4. **Final Total / Grand Summary Rows**:
   - Top border: 1px solid dark gray (`#424242`).
   - Bottom border: Classic accounting **double underline** (2 parallel lines).

---

## 3. Alignment Matrix

Misaligned columns are the #1 sign of amateur work. Follow this strict matrix:

| Data Type | Horizontal Alignment | Vertical Alignment | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text Labels / Descriptions | Left | Middle | "Gross Profit" |
| Numeric Quantities & Dollars | Right | Middle | "$1,450,200" |
| Dates & Period Codes | Center | Middle | "2026-03-31" or "Q1" |
| Status / Category Badges | Center | Middle | "COMPLETED", "ACTIVE" |
| Percentages & Margins | Right | Middle | "64.2%" |
| Notes & Annotations | Left | Top | "Excludes one-off restructuring costs" |
